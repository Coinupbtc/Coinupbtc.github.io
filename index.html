Analyze

Control charts vs ML on real fab data

secom-spc-vs-ai

I ran control charts and ML against each other on real fab data: 1,567 production runs and 590 sensor signals from UCI SECOM, per the README and the committed JSON. At a calibrated 1% false-alarm budget on a time-ordered holdout, no method reliably beat chance (every AUROC 95% interval includes 0.5). Phase I limits over-alarmed 1.8–4.2× their 1% target on later passes, as I wrote up in the README.

Numbers are in the results JSON, the write-up is the README, and the preregistration was written before the test block was scored.

Improve

One change, measured

I turned thinking off on the client (enable_thinking=false) and cut wall-clock per request from up to 98 s to 15 s or less, with no drop on my 4-prompt quality suite — though that suite tops out at 9/10, so it can't see small losses. It was a speed-for-reasoning trade-off I chose for interactive use, not a pure improvement — see the committed pass table. The serving flags I tried after (flash attention, KV q8_0, context size, slot count) each moved decode under 2%, also in the committed pass table.
